# PokerGame 1.1.0 — Learn, practice, and replay your hands

PokerGame now gives you a way to learn the basics and revisit the decisions you made at the table. This update adds an offline learning and practice flow alongside free play.

- **Seven interactive lessons:** work through the table, hand combinations, actions, and settlement. Tutorial progress is saved locally, and you can repeat completed lessons.
- **Set up your table:** choose 1–5 opponents, three difficulty levels, 1,000/2,000/5,000/10,000 starting chips, and four fixed blind levels.
- **Practice at your own pace:** pause after each hand, or let the next hand begin automatically after settlement.
- **Replay completed hands:** step through actions, autoplay, jump to streets, and switch between the original perspective and an all-cards view. The all-cards view is available only after the hand ends.
- **Keep track of your play:** browse hand records, local statistics, and achievements. Up to 1,000 full hand histories can be retained; deleting a history does not erase cumulative statistics.
- **Updated opponents:** AI decisions now use starting-hand ranges and estimates based on public actions, with bounded postflop simulations. These are heuristic opponents, not a claim of optimal poker play.
- **More reliable record saving:** failed saves show a retry option, and replaying a hand does not count it twice.

The coach panel does not yet provide advice or win probabilities, and radar ratings are not implemented. A match in progress still cannot be resumed after quitting. If a save fails, retry before closing the game.

Download the new Windows x64 or macOS Universal ZIP and extract it into a fresh folder. Existing local settings remain in the same application data directory; the new practice records are stored alongside them. The interface remains Simplified Chinese. No account, internet connection, real-money wagering, or external AI service is required.

Windows builds are unsigned. macOS builds use ad-hoc signing and are not Apple-notarized; Intel Macs have not been separately validated. Automated checks and exported-package self-tests do not establish a new target-device graphical test matrix. Use the accompanying SHA-256 files to verify downloads.

If you encounter a confusing hand or a bug, please include your version, operating system, table settings, and the steps that led to it in an [issue report](https://github.com/Jerryszz02/pokerGame/issues).
