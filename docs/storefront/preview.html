Your seat at the table is always open.

A free, single-player Texas Hold’em game. Play in your browser or download for offline play on Windows and macOS. Sit down with up to five AI opponents in a lamp-lit pixel-art poker room. Read the table, choose your moment, and play for the last chip.

Single player · 1–5 AI opponents · Four difficulty levels

Language: Choose English or 简体中文 in Settings, with separate music and sound volumes.

Download for Windows or macOS · Or launch the browser game above

New in 1.3.0: know your play, choose your challenge.

Choose opponent personalities, try Hell difficulty, view local coaching hints, review your decisions with numbers and offline written feedback, and explore your six-axis player radar. Browser play, English / 简体中文 switching, and table audio are included. Click or press a key to enable browser audio.

Learn, practice, replay.

Start with seven interactive tutorials, play a free match, or use practice mode to pause between hands. Choose your starting stack and blinds, revisit completed hands step by step, and track local statistics and achievements.

Local coaching, numerical replay analysis, rule-based written reviews and player-style statistics work offline. Cloud replay explanations need a connection and fall back to local feedback when unavailable; only the local analysis summary is sent. The radar describes your play and sample counts, not a skill rating. The browser build needs a connection to load.

Call the bet. Read the room.

Two cards in your hand. Five on the table. Fold, call, raise, or go all-in as each hand moves from the blinds to the showdown. All-ins, side pots, and split pots are handled for you.

Pick your opponents. Set your pace.

  • Heads-up or a full table: choose one to five AI opponents and Easy, Medium, Hard, or Hell difficulty.
  • Different betting personalities: on Medium, Hard or Hell, choose a named style for the whole table, random styles for individual opponents, or the difficulty default.
  • Play for the whole table: choose 1,000–10,000 starting chips and one of four fixed blind levels. Win all the chips to finish the match.
  • Take your time: choose normal or fast actions, pause when you need a break, and open the current hand’s action log.
  • Keep it local: no game account, real-money wagering, or background data collection. Desktop downloads work offline. Settings and completed-hand statistics stay on your device.

One more hand?

See the cards turn over and the pot find its winner. Then take the next hand—or change the table and start again.

Before you take a seat

Browser play, plus Windows x64 and macOS desktop downloads. For browser play, try desktop Chrome or Edge with WebGL 2.0 and SharedArrayBuffer support; download the desktop version if your browser is unsupported. Use a mouse and a display area of at least 1280 × 720. No Godot editor or .NET installation is needed. The macOS package includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries; Intel Macs have not been separately validated.

First launch: Windows builds are unsigned; macOS builds are not Apple-notarized. Your system may show a security prompt. Read the download instructions and release notes.

Saving: settings, tutorial progress, completed-hand records, statistics, and achievements are saved locally. Browser saves stay in the current browser profile and device, and do not sync with desktop saves or an itch.io account. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove access to progress. An unfinished match is not saved when you return to the menu, refresh, or quit.
