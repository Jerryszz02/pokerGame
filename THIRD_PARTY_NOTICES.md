## Game artwork and audio

The game uses the generated artwork documented in `assets/art/generated/README.md`.
Card values, suits, chip amounts, action labels and results are rendered from game state.
Background music: **Airport Lounge** by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com),
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
[Original work](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100806).
The MP3 is unmodified; playback repeats at reduced volume.
Attribution and source details: `assets/licenses/Airport-Lounge.txt`.

Card and chip sounds: [Casino Audio by Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio),
CC0. Five unmodified OGG files are bundled in `assets/audio/`.
The author's license is in `assets/licenses/Kenney-Casino-Audio.txt`.
These license files are also included in desktop release packages.

These notices concern dependencies and asset provenance; they do not assign a new
