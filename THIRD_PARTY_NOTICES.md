## Game artwork and audio

The game uses the generated artwork documented in `assets/art/generated/README.md`.
Card values, suits, chip amounts, action labels and results are rendered from game state.
Action tones are synthesized locally. No music track is included.

These notices concern dependencies and asset provenance; they do not assign a new
license to this project's original code or artwork.
